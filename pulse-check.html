Observe your pulse.

A pulse measurement cannot identify a cancer type or rule out cancer.

This is an informational research prototype, not a medical device. A pulse result does not assess lung tissue or replace clinical evaluation. No recording, pulse waveform or result is uploaded or saved by this page.

Option 1 · Manual wrist pulse

Place your index and middle fingers gently on the thumb side of your other wrist. The phone provides a timer; it does not sense pressure or measure your wrist itself. Count the beats you feel for the full 60 seconds, then enter your count.

Keep breathing naturally. Cancel if you lose count and start again.

Ready when you are.

For automatic camera monitoring, use a fingertip rather than a manual wrist count. This does not turn the phone into a wrist sensor.

Option 2 · Experimental fingertip camera

On a compatible phone, gently cover the rear camera with your fingertip and keep still. This method is not configured or validated for holding a phone against your wrist. Camera and light availability vary by device. Use the manual option if it cannot obtain a clear signal.

Do not press hard. Stop if the phone becomes warm or uncomfortable. The estimate takes about 25 seconds; camera permission is requested only after you press the button.

Keep the phone comfortable and stop if it becomes warm. Monitoring ends when you stop it, change modes, or leave the page.

Option 3 · Experimental neck rPPG

Find good, steady diffuse lighting and rest the device on a stable surface. Keep the camera away from your neck: this is non-contact video. Leave a small patch of neck skin visible, hold still and breathe naturally. No pressure on your neck is needed.

You select the region manually. This app does not automatically identify the neck or verify anatomy. Ordinary RGB-camera neck accuracy has not been established for this implementation.

Check the local-processing consent box, then press Start neck camera. Allow camera access if your browser asks.

Camera is off. Your live neck preview will appear here.

Drag a rectangle over visible neck skin in the preview. If you cannot select a region, use the manual wrist option.

Camera-controlled focus. The app cannot locate an artery or see an internal pulse point.

Lighting and movement checks appear after selecting a region.
