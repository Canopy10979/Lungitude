Five imaging dimensions

01 · Asymmetry & borders

Reflection mismatch and perimeter circularity from your outline.

02 · Color variation

RGB variation and saturation/brightness spread. Lighting changes affect these values.

03 · Texture

Gray-level co-occurrence contrast, homogeneity and energy; no surface elevation measurement.

04 · Vascular architecture

Requires dermoscopic imaging

No validated vessel model is installed.

05 · Temporal evolution

Dated follow-up required

Compare separate visits with standardized scale and camera conditions. A short recording does not measure growth.
