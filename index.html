Camera won’t open? Use your iPhone camera or Photos.

Live camera access needs HTTPS on your iPhone. You can also choose a photo, or a video up to 20 seconds / 50 MB and capture a frame from it.

Recording and frame selection happen on your device. No audio. Only your selected still image is sent when you explicitly request model analysis. Recordings are not uploaded.
