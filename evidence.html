Breast measurement research

The separate breast model requires all 30 Wisconsin sampled-cell measurements. It does not analyze breast photographs or recordings. Its internal held-out evaluation had 114 records: 72 true negatives, 41 true positives, one false negative and zero false positives. This is not external or clinical validation.

Open breast measurement research. Skin scores and breast classifications are not fused because they concern different inputs and target conditions.

What the skin workflow actually does

  • Captures an eight-second local recording or a still image.
  • Retains up to three frames ranked by an experimental sharpness heuristic.
  • Sends only a selected still image after separate consent.
  • Shows research class scores only when a compatible trained skin checkpoint is installed.

It does not automatically verify that skin or a lesion is present. It does not infer growth from an eight-second recording. An image-category score is not your probability of cancer. Skin-tone, lighting, camera and site differences need independent evaluation.

Before we claim performance

  1. Train a patient-separated PAD baseline and compare validation macro F1, per-class recall and latency.
  2. Evaluate on a separate appropriately labeled clinical-photo cohort; never tune to the test set.
  3. Test real iPhones for permissions, focus, orientation, recording formats and interruptions.
  4. Report failed captures and subgroup performance, not just successful images.

Clinical concern takes precedence over model output. A new, different, changing, itching or bleeding spot should be discussed with a dermatologist.
